## Tone principles for every image

- No neon crypto candles
- No 3D-rendered coins
- No Lamborghini imagery
- No lens flares
- No emoji
- No display fonts (Comic Sans, Impact, etc.)
- Only two colors: electric green + one neutral (near-black or cream)
- Warm, technical, calm — the tool waits
